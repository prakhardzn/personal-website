## About the project

This project is a title-sequence experiment based on Zoo by Bert Haanstra.

## The project

The sequence takes inspiration from the observational humour of the film and translates that tone into motion, imagery and typography.

## Process

I worked with timing, visual repetition and typographic movement to create a sequence that felt playful without becoming disconnected from the original film.

## What I learned

The project helped me understand how motion, typography and editing rhythm can establish the personality of a film before the main narrative begins.
