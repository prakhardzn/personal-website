## About the project

This project explores typography through a poster inspired by the visual world and atmosphere of Dune.

## The project

The poster focuses on scale, hierarchy, spacing and cinematic tension rather than relying only on imagery from the film.

## Process

I experimented with type size, composition and spatial relationships to create a sense of monumentality and distance.

## What I learned

The project helped me understand how typography alone can establish atmosphere, hierarchy and visual rhythm.
