## About the project

Kshitij is a short film exploring photography, consent and the relationship between the person behind the camera and the person being photographed.

## My role

I worked as the writer and director of the film.

## The project

The story follows a student photographer whose interaction with a balloon seller raises questions about observation, representation and the assumptions we make about people through images.

## Process

The project involved developing the screenplay, directing performances and using photography itself as an important part of the narrative.

## What I learned

Kshitij made me think more carefully about consent, representation and the responsibility that comes with documenting another person.
