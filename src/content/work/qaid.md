## About the project

Qaid is a psychological thriller about a man trapped inside a repeating cycle where memory, time and reality begin collapsing into one another.

## My role

I worked as the director and director of photography on the film.

## The project

The film explores repetition, memory and the feeling of being unable to escape a sequence of events that keeps returning.

## Process

The project involved developing the story, planning the visual language, directing the performances and designing repeated moments so that each return to the loop felt familiar while still moving the story forward.

## What I learned

Qaid became an exercise in visual storytelling, continuity and maintaining tension across repeated situations.
