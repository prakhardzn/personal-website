## About the project

Street Photography is an ongoing collection of photographs documenting people, movement and everyday situations in public spaces.

## The project

The collection focuses on ordinary moments that become visually interesting through gesture, light, colour, layering or coincidence.

## Process

Most of the photographs are made while walking through public spaces and observing situations without heavily staging or controlling them.

## What I am exploring

I am interested in how small interactions, visual layers and unexpected moments can reveal something about a place and the people occupying it.

## Status

This collection is still growing and will continue to change as new photographs are added.
